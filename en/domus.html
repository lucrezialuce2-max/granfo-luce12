L'eleganza del Murano glass

Light has never felt so tangible. Domus is born from the meeting of the ancient tradition of kiln-fired glass and a contemporary design Based on pure interlocking.

Each lamp is made of artistic glass tiles, handcrafted to highlight textures and imperfections that make every piece unique. The real magic is freedom: the plates are not fixed, you can compose and recompose them to create ever-new volumes and color plays.
