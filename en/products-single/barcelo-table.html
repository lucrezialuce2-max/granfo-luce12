Barceló Table Lamp

Geometric Brightness Control

Barceló reinterprets the classic table lamp for contemporary living with refined simplicity. Featuring a black square base and pristine white shade, this fixture balances form and function with understated elegance. The four dimmable G9 sockets provide exceptional versatility—from concentrated task lighting for focused work to soft ambient illumination for evening relaxation.

Perfect for desks, nightstands, console tables, and lounge areas, Barceló seamlessly adapts to residential and professional environments. The geometric purity of its design complements minimalist, mid-century modern, and contemporary interiors. Four individually dimmable sources allow unprecedented control over luminous mood, transforming any surface into a dynamic lighting environment.

Technical Specifications

  • Type: Table Lamp
  • Material: Painted metal, textile shade
  • Dimensions: Contact us for specifications
  • Socket Type: 4x G9 dimmable
  • Available Finishes: Black base, white shade
  • Light Type: Warm/Cold (depending on bulb)
