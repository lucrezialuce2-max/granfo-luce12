Sospensione Piatto

Flat Form Minimalism

Sospensione Piatto embraces extreme minimalism through its flat plate form. Reducing the suspension to its essential geometry, this fixture creates dramatic visual impact through subtraction rather than addition. The plate shape produces distinctive light patterns and shadows, transforming architecture through pure form and functional elegance without unnecessary ornamentation.

Perfect for modern offices, contemporary galleries, minimalist residences, and architectural spaces celebrating purity, Sospensione Piatto makes bold design statements through simplicity. The flat form creates unexpected light dynamics while maintaining maximum visual lightness. Ideal for purists seeking lighting that achieves maximum impact through minimum means.

Technical Specifications

  • Type: Suspension Lamp
  • Material: Metal
  • Dimensions: Contact us for specifications
  • Socket Type: E27
  • Available Finishes: Multiple finishes available on request
  • Light Type: Warm/Cold (depending on bulb)
