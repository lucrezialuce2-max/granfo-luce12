LumiNero Suspension Lamp

Minimalist Black Metal Elegance

LumiNero represents the pinnacle of contemporary design—a bold black metal suspension that commands attention through its refined simplicity. With a generous 40cm diameter, this fixture offers substantial visual impact while maintaining an understated elegance. The direct light source ensures efficient illumination, making it ideal for both functional and aesthetic applications in modern interiors.

Perfect for dining areas, foyers, and modern living spaces, LumiNero transforms any room with its geometric purity. The matte black finish absorbs light beautifully, creating subtle depth and sophistication. Whether suspended alone as a statement piece or grouped in multiples for dynamic visual composition, this lamp adapts to contemporary architectural visions while remaining timeless.

Technical Specifications

  • Type: Suspension Lamp
  • Material: Painted Metal
  • Dimensions: 40cm diameter
  • Socket Type: GU10
  • Available Finishes: Matte Black
  • Light Type: Direct
