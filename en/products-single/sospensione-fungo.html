Sospensione Fungo

Organic Mushroom Form

Sospensione Fungo draws inspiration from nature's sculptural forms. The mushroom silhouette creates a distinctive organic shape that brings warmth and whimsy to contemporary spaces. This suspension fixture celebrates the intersection of nature and design, where curved forms and soft proportions create inviting illumination that enhances rather than dominates its surroundings.

Perfect for modern kitchens, dining areas, artistic spaces, and homes celebrating biophilic design, Sospensione Fungo adds personality and charm. The organic form creates playful focal points while maintaining practical illumination, making it ideal for families and creatives who appreciate nature-inspired design. Each installation brings a smile—design that delights through form.

Technical Specifications

  • Type: Suspension Lamp
  • Material: Metal, Textile
  • Dimensions: Contact us for specifications
  • Socket Type: E27
  • Available Finishes: Multiple finishes available on request
  • Light Type: Warm/Cold (depending on bulb)
