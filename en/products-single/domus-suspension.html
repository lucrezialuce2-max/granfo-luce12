Domus Suspension

Transparent Murano Transparency

Domus celebrates the purity and clarity of transparent Murano glass, available in various rich colors that capture light with crystalline brilliance. This suspension fixture embodies contemporary elegance with its distinctive form that reveals the intricate craftsmanship inherent to traditional Venetian glass-making. The E27 socket ensures practical compatibility while maintaining artistic integrity.

Perfect for dining rooms, modern kitchens, loft spaces, and contemporary residences, Domus creates vibrant plays of light and color throughout the day. The transparent glass allows illumination to pass through with clarity, casting colorful shadows and luminous patterns that enliven architectural spaces. Each color option offers a distinct personality while maintaining the fixture's minimalist elegance.

Technical Specifications

  • Type: Suspension Lamp
  • Material: Transparent Murano Glass
  • Dimensions: Contact us for specifications
  • Socket Type: E27
  • Available Finishes: Multiple colors available on request
  • Light Type: Warm/Cold (depending on bulb)
