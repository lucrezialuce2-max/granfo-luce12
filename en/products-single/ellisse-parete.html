Ellisse Wall Applique

Organic Elliptical Elegance

Ellisse celebrates curved geometry with contemporary refinement. The elliptical form softens rigid architectural lines while creating visual flow and movement across wall compositions. This wall applique bridges organic and minimalist design languages, offering elegant illumination that humanizes modern spaces through gently rounded proportions and intuitive visual rhythm.

Perfect for living room walls, bedroom accents, gallery spaces, and contemporary residences, Ellisse creates soft light diffusion with architectural sophistication. The elliptical proportion complements both minimalist and organic interior design approaches, while the focused illumination pattern highlights artwork or architectural features with subtle grace. An ideal choice for those seeking warmth through form.

Technical Specifications

  • Type: Wall Applique
  • Material: Painted Metal, Glass
  • Dimensions: Contact us for specifications
  • Socket Type: E27 or G9
  • Available Finishes: Multiple finishes available on request
  • Light Type: Warm/Cold (depending on bulb)
