Barra Wall Applique

Linear Minimalist Design

Barra distills wall lighting to its essential elements—a contemporary fixture that speaks through geometric simplicity and refined elegance. The linear form creates dynamic visual rhythm along corridors and accent walls while providing efficient, focused illumination. With its minimalist aesthetic, Barra adapts seamlessly to modern architecture and contemporary interior spaces.

Ideal for hallways, office walls, accent lighting, and residential corridors, Barra bridges decorative and functional illumination. The clean proportions create visual interest without overwhelming adjacent surfaces, while the focused light pattern highlights architectural features and guides movement through spaces. Perfect for those seeking understated sophistication.

Technical Specifications

  • Type: Wall Applique
  • Material: Painted Metal, Glass
  • Dimensions: Contact us for specifications
  • Socket Type: E27 or G9
  • Available Finishes: Multiple finishes available on request
  • Light Type: Warm/Cold (depending on bulb)
