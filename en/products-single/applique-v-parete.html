Applique V Wall

Angular Contemporary Statement

Applique V embraces angular geometry with bold contemporary vision. The distinctive V-shaped form creates dynamic visual movement and architectural interest while providing directional illumination with sculptural impact. This wall fixture makes a confident design statement, perfect for those seeking lighting that expresses personality and contemporary sensibility through geometric precision.

Ideal for accent walls, modern living spaces, contemporary galleries, and architectural feature lighting, Applique V creates dramatic shadows and light play that evolve with time of day and viewing angle. The angular proportions command attention while maintaining functional illumination, making it perfect for those who view lighting as sculptural art. A bold choice for expressive interiors.

Technical Specifications

  • Type: Wall Applique
  • Material: Painted Metal, Glass
  • Dimensions: Contact us for specifications
  • Socket Type: E27 or G9
  • Available Finishes: Multiple finishes available on request
  • Light Type: Warm/Cold (depending on bulb)
