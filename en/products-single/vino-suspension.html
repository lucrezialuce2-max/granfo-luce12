Vino Suspension

Sophisticated Glass Whimsy

Vino Suspension celebrates the elegant form of the wine glass in suspended form. This glass pendant merges functional lighting with sophisticated whimsy, creating a conversation piece that delights through unexpected form language. The transparent glass captures light beautifully while the wine glass silhouette brings personality and joy to dining and entertaining spaces.

Perfect for wine bars, dining rooms, sophisticated kitchens, and entertaining spaces, Vino Suspension creates memorable focal points. The playful form celebrates life's pleasures—gathering, conversation, and the ritual of sharing food and wine. Each installation becomes a witty design statement, elevating ordinary lighting into artistic expression that brings smiles to guests and residents alike.

  • Material: Glass, Metal
  • Dimensions: Contact us for specifications
