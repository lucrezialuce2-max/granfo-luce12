Sospensione Tubo

Industrial Tubular Elegance

Sospensione Tubo celebrates industrial design heritage through cylindrical form and authentic materials. This suspension fixture channels mid-century industrial aesthetics while maintaining contemporary refinement. The tubular geometry creates clean sight lines and functional elegance, perfect for spaces embracing honest design where form follows function without compromise.

Perfect for industrial lofts, modern offices, cafes, contemporary studios, and design-conscious homes, Sospensione Tubo creates sophisticated focal points. The tubular proportions adapt to minimalist and industrial aesthetics equally well. Each installation celebrates authenticity and functional beauty—lighting that speaks through honest form rather than ornamentation.

Technical Specifications

  • Type: Suspension Lamp
  • Material: Metal, Powder-coated finish
  • Dimensions: Contact us for specifications
  • Socket Type: E27
  • Available Finishes: Multiple finishes available on request
  • Light Type: Warm/Cold (depending on bulb)
