Bolla Suspension

Perfect Sphere Luminescence

Bolla Suspension celebrates the perfect geometry of the sphere. This glass bubble suspension captures light with crystalline beauty, creating a luminous orb that floats gracefully from the ceiling. The spherical form represents timeless elegance—an organic perfect form through which light dances, diffuses, and creates ever-changing visual poetry throughout the day and evening.

Perfect for modern dining areas, contemporary lofts, artistic spaces, and sophisticated residences, Bolla Suspension creates focal points through organic geometry. The transparent glass allows light to flow through with remarkable clarity while the spherical form ensures even 360-degree illumination. Ideal for those seeking lighting that elevates spaces through pure geometric beauty.

Technical Specifications

  • Material: Glass, Metal
  • Dimensions: Contact us for specifications
